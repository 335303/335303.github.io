# 02 - Go-To-Market & Product Strategy (AuraVision G1 - Germany)

## Positioning
- Position **AuraVision G1** around **privacy-first clarity, workplace readiness, and prescription/optical comfort** rather than covert recording or social media novelty.
- Recommended pricing tier anchor: **€549** for the core professional/business configuration, supported by pilot bundles for German enterprise and optical partners.

## Go-To-Market (GTM) Channels
1. **Enterprise & Industrial Pilots:** Target engineering, manufacturing, maintenance, and logistics teams seeking hands-free guidance and transparent compliance controls.
2. **Optician & Optical Retail Partnerships:** Collaborate with optical specialists to address prescription lenses, fit, and everyday wearability.

## Privacy & Trust Architecture
- Default-off sensitive sensors, explicit visual status indicators, transparent data governance, and enterprise policy controls to simplify *Betriebsrat* and DSGVO reviews.

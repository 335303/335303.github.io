# 01 - Market Research: Smart Glasses in Germany (AuraVision G1)

## Market Overview & Growth
- The German smart glasses market is projected to grow at a strong compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of roughly 28%, expanding from ~$68M in 2024 toward ~$299.3M by 2029.
- Enterprise and industrial use cases (remote assistance, guided assembly, logistics, field service, and hands-free workflows) represent the primary near-term commercial opportunity.

## Consumer & Optical Dynamics
- German consumers exhibit high sensitivity toward privacy, surveillance, and unannounced audio/video capture in public and workplace environments.
- Prescription eyewear plays a central role in Germany, with optical retail channels and prescription compatibility strongly influencing adoption and trust.

## Legal & Workplace Compliance
- **GDPR (DSGVO):** Clear legal basis, transparency, data minimization, and explicit consent/controls are essential when handling personal or workplace data.
- **Audio & Recording Restrictions (StGB § 201):** Unauthorized recording of private spoken words carries severe legal penalties in Germany.
- **Works Council (Betriebsrat / BetrVG § 87):** Workplace deployment of monitoring-capable or sensor-equipped tools requires alignment with works council co-determination rules and data protection assessments.
